# Kariakoo field research (Dnols)

This brief is the v1 stand-in for on-the-ground interviews. It is written so the product can ship the **pay-then-reveal, inspect-and-reject** loop without waiting for a two-week stall visit. Re-run the script below in Kariakoo before scaling past the first cluster.

## Method

- **Place:** Kariakoo, Dar es Salaam (fashion stalls + phone/electronics rows).
- **Target:** 15 buyers + 15 shops (fashion stall, shoe stall, phone box, accessories).
- **Prompt:** Would you pay the **full price into escrow** before seeing the item, if you can refuse at the counter and get a refund? What deposit % if not? Who sends you customers today (dalali, WhatsApp, walk-up)?

## Secondary findings (Tanzania / East Africa, 2025–2026)

- **Jiji / Facebook / WhatsApp** own discovery and chat. Ghosting, bait-and-switch, and cash at the stall are normal. No live stock, no escrow for walk-in.
- **Jumia** exited Tanzania in 2019; a return is not a 2026 local competitor.
- **Kariakoo Africa** is the closest analogue: digitize stalls, **pay then deliver**, vendor paid ~48h. Dnols is **pay then walk**, pin hidden until pay.
- **JumlaJumla / Msosi** is delivery and food, not stall discovery.
- Buyers already compare prices by walking. Shops fear window-shoppers who take a price then buy next door. **Hiding the pin until pay** matches that shop anxiety.
- Buyers will resist paying for a dress or a phone they have not seen. Policy for v1: **full price held in escrow + refuse at pickup** (not a non-refundable deposit). Pickup SLA **2 hours** so stock is not frozen overnight.
- Mobile money (M-Pesa, Mixx by Yas, Airtel Money) is the rail. Cards are secondary. Do not issue an in-app wallet.

## Working hypotheses to validate on the stall

| Hypothesis | Pass if |
| --- | --- |
| Fashion buyers accept escrow if they can refuse fit/fabric | ≥8/15 say yes to full escrow + refuse |
| Phone buyers want IMEI / power-on at handover | ≥8/15 name fake/IMEI as the fear |
| Shops accept 2-hour hold | ≥8/15 shops ok with a timed reserve |
| QR at a market gate beats Play Store | Buyers will scan a poster without an app install |

## Script (Swahili / English)

1. How did you find this stall last time you bought here?
2. If an app showed “300 m away” but not the stall until you paid, would you pay?
3. Full hold vs 20% deposit — which would you use?
4. What would make you refuse at the counter (size, fake phone, price change)?
5. Shop: who is a dalali to you, and would you pay a platform instead?

Until field notes replace this file, the app implements hypothesis **full escrow + refuse**, not deposit-first.
